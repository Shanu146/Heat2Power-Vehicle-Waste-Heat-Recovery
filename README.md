# 🔋 Heat2Power – Vehicle Waste Heat Recovery

### Converting Vehicle Waste Heat into Electrical Energy ⚡

## 📌 About the Project

Heat2Power is a thermoelectric energy harvesting system designed to convert waste heat from vehicle engines and silencers into electrical energy using Thermoelectric Generator (TEG) modules.

The system integrates ESP32-based monitoring, temperature sensing, voltage and current measurement, and battery charging management to utilize recovered thermal energy for auxiliary applications.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Recover waste heat from vehicle engines and silencers.
- Convert thermal energy into electrical energy using TEG modules.
- Monitor temperature, voltage and current in real time.
- Store harvested energy in a 12V battery.
- Develop a sustainable and detachable vehicle energy harvesting solution.

## ⚙️ Hardware Components

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| SP1848 TEG Modules | Thermoelectric energy generation |
| MLX90614 IR Sensor | Non-contact hot-side temperature measurement |
| ESP32 | Embedded monitoring and control |
| INA226 Sensor | Voltage and current measurement |
| XH-M604 | Battery charging control |
| ESP32-S3 3.5-inch Touch Display | Wireless monitoring interface |
| 12V Lead-Acid Battery | Energy storage |

## 🔬 Working Principle

1. TEG modules capture heat from the vehicle engine or silencer.
2. The Seebeck effect converts the temperature difference into DC electricity.
3. INA226 measures the generated voltage and current.
4. MLX90614 monitors the hot-side temperature.
5. ESP32 processes sensor data and enables wireless communication.
6. The XH-M604 manages battery charging.
7. Generated energy is stored in a 12V lead-acid battery.
